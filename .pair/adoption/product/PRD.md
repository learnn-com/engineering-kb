# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Overview

**Product Name:** Learnn
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Date:** 2026-02-10
**Owner:** Learnn SRL

### Executive Summary

Learnn is the continuous digital upskilling system for working professionals who need to stay relevant in the digital economy without the risk, cost, and time commitment of traditional masters or MBAs. We replace the outdated "master every few years" logic with an always-on, guided learning system combining 400+ expert-taught courses with AI-powered support.

## 2. Product Vision & Mission

### Vision

Replace traditional masters and MBAs as the go-to solution for professionals who need to stay credible, useful, and competitive in their roles as work continuously evolves in the digital and AI era.

### Mission

We deliver a low-risk, high-quality alternative to expensive, static master programs by providing continuous access to expert-led digital education, structured learning paths, and AI-powered guidance — enabling professionals to stay always covered without career interruptions or financial strain.

## 3. Problem Statement & Market Definition

### Market

**Digitalizzazione continua della carriera** — the continuous upskilling market for working professionals who cannot afford to pause their careers or make high-risk educational investments.

We are the modern alternative to:
- Traditional masters (€10-20k, 1-2 years, static content)
- Executive MBAs (€30-100k, rigid schedules, outdated curriculum)
- Expensive corporate training programs (fragmented, no continuity)

### The High-Urgency Decision We Solve

**Target Decision Moment:**
- *"Sto per investire €10-20k in un master e ho paura di buttare soldi e tempo"*
- *"Devo aggiornarmi su digitale e AI per restare rilevante, ma non posso fermarmi due anni per studiare"*
- *"Non posso permettermi di comprare 30 corsi random o affidarmi a YouTube e ChatGPT senza guida"*

These are not abstract fears — these are professionals actively researching:
- Master programs
- Executive education
- Corporate training
- Expensive consultants/agencies
- Individual courses (€500-3000 each)

### The Clear Enemy (What We're Positioned Against)

1. **Traditional Masters & MBAs:**
   - Static content (outdated by graduation)
   - High cost (€10-100k)
   - Time commitment (1-2 years full/part-time)
   - Career interruption risk

2. **Expensive Individual Courses:**
   - Fragmented learning (no structured path)
   - High per-course cost (€500-3000)
   - No ongoing support
   - Quickly outdated

3. **Fragmented Free Learning (YouTube, ChatGPT, blogs):**
   - No guidance or structure
   - Quality inconsistent
   - Time-consuming to curate
   - No credentials or verification

### Core Pain Points

- **High-Risk Investment:** Professionals cannot afford to waste €10-20k on a master that may become irrelevant before graduation
- **Time Scarcity:** Working professionals cannot pause careers for 1-2 years to complete traditional programs
- **Content Obsolescence:** Digital/AI landscape changes faster than traditional education can update (6-12 month curriculum cycles vs weekly industry changes)
- **Lack of Ongoing Support:** One-time courses leave users alone when applying knowledge to real work scenarios
- **Fragmentation Paralysis:** Overwhelmed by countless course options, no clear path forward

## 4. Goals & Success Metrics

### Primary Goals

1. **Position as Master/MBA Alternative:** Be the first solution professionals evaluate when considering expensive educational investments
2. **Reduce Formation Risk:** Lower decision risk from €10-20k (master) to €200-400/year (Learnn subscription)
3. **Enable Continuous Learning:** Replace "upskill every X years" with "always covered" mentality
4. **Deliver Verifiable Outcomes:** Provide credentials, certifications, and AI-powered application support that keep users competitive

### Success Metrics (KPIs)

**Business Metrics:**
- **MRR Growth:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (target: €100k→€500k in 2026)
- **Subscriber Growth:** Active paying subscribers (target: 1000→5000 in 2026)
- **Churn Rate:** Monthly subscriber cancellations (target: <5%)
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** Average revenue per subscriber over lifetime (target: €1200+)
- **Conversion Rate:** Free users → Paid subscribers (target: ≥15%)

**Product Metrics:**
- **Course Completion Rate:** Percentage of users completing courses (target: ≥40%)
- **Certification Rate:** Users obtaining verified certificates (target: ≥30%)
- **Learnn AI Adoption:** Active AI assistant users (target: ≥60% of subscribers)
- **Weekly Engagement:** Average hours spent learning per week (target: ≥2 hours)
- **Path Completion:** Users completing structured master paths (target: ≥25%)

**Outcome Metrics:**
- **Perceived Value:** Users reporting career advancement or skills applied to work (target: ≥70%)
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score):** User satisfaction and recommendation likelihood (target: ≥50)
- **Master Alternative Positioning:** % of users who considered Learnn instead of traditional master (target: ≥40%)

## 5. Target Users (ICP - Ideal Customer Profile)

### Primary Persona: "Ambitious Working Professional"

**Demographics:**
- **Age:** 28-45 years old
- **Role:** Manager, consultant, entrepreneur, business/marketing/operations professional
- **Technical Level:** Non-technical (business-focused, not software engineers)
- **Income:** Stable mid-to-high income (€35k-80k/year)
- **Employment:** Currently working full-time, cannot pause career

**Psychographics:**
- **Ambitious:** Career-driven, want to stay ahead and competitive
- **Risk-Averse:** High fear of making wrong educational investment decisions
- **Time-Constrained:** No capacity for 1-2 year full-time programs
- **Value-Conscious:** Will invest in education but demand clear ROI
- **Self-Directed:** Prefer autonomy over rigid schedules

**Current Situation:**
- Actively evaluating a master program or serious educational investment (€5-20k range)
- Feeling pressure to upskill in digital/AI but unsure of best path
- Previously purchased individual courses but felt overwhelmed or unsupported
- Concerned about content becoming outdated quickly in fast-changing fields

**Behavioral Indicators:**
- Searches: "master marketing digitale", "MBA alternativa", "corso AI per manager"
- Consumes: LinkedIn Learning, YouTube tutorials, podcasts on digital trends
- Networks: Follows industry experts, attends webinars, active on LinkedIn
- Invests: Willing to pay €200-400/year if risk is mitigated and value is clear

### Secondary Persona: "Corporate Team Lead"

- Same profile as primary but manages a team
- Budget to upskill team members (€500-1000/person/year)
- Seeks scalable solution for continuous team development
- Future opportunity: Corporate/team subscriptions

### User Journey (Decision-Focused)

**Stage 1: Evaluation Crisis (High Urgency)**
- Professional realizes they're falling behind in digital/AI skills
- Researches masters, executive programs (€10-20k sticker shock)
- Discovers Learnn via ads, search, or word-of-mouth

**Stage 2: Risk Assessment**
- Explores Learnn catalog, reads expert bios, watches preview lessons
- Compares: Master (€15k, 2 years, static) vs Learnn (€300/year, continuous, updated weekly)
- Key decision factor: *"Can I stay covered without gambling €15k?"*

**Stage 3: Low-Risk Trial**
- Subscribes to Learnn (monthly plan, €29/month) — much lower risk than master
- Gains immediate access to 400+ courses, 10+ master paths

**Stage 4: Structured Learning**
- Follows guided master path (e.g., "AI Marketing Master")
- Watches courses, completes quizzes, downloads templates
- Uses Learnn AI for personalized guidance and work application

**Stage 5: Verification & Confidence**
- Earns verified certifications, shares on LinkedIn
- Applies skills to real work scenarios with AI support
- Realizes continuous value: new content weekly, always up-to-date

**Stage 6: Retention & Advocacy**
- Continues subscription (€29/month or €299/year)
- Becomes advocate: *"Learnn saved me from wasting €15k on an outdated master"*
- Refers colleagues and team members

## 6. Solution Overview

### What We Really Sell

**Not:** A course platform
**But:** A continuous, guided, low-risk system for acquiring and maintaining digital/AI competence while you work.

**We replace:** The logic "I do a master every X years"
**With:** "I'm always covered" — continuous learning that evolves with your career.

### Core Value Proposition

**For working professionals evaluating expensive master programs,**
**Learnn is the continuous digital upskilling system**
**That provides expert-led education, structured paths, and AI-powered guidance**
**Unlike traditional masters, we keep you always updated without career interruption or financial risk.**

### Key Differentiators (Why Us)

1. **Practitioners, Not Professors:**
   - Content taught by active professionals who work in the field today
   - Real-world application, not academic theory
   - Examples and templates from actual client work

2. **Continuous Updates (Kills Traditional Masters):**
   - New courses added weekly
   - Content refreshed as industry evolves
   - No 6-12 month curriculum lag like masters

3. **Guided Paths, Not Catalog Overload:**
   - Structured master paths with clear progression
   - No decision paralysis from 10,000+ course catalogs (like Udemy)
   - Clear "start here → end here" journey

4. **AI + Human Support When You Need It:**
   - Learnn AI trained on expert content for personalized guidance
   - Apply knowledge to your specific work scenarios
   - Reduce time from "learning" to "doing"

5. **Drastically Lower Cost & Risk:**
   - €299/year vs €10-20k for master
   - Try monthly (€29) before committing to annual
   - Cancel anytime — no 1-2 year lock-in

### Solution Components

#### Must-Have (P0) - Current

1. **400+ Expert-Led Courses:**
   - Marketing, AI, business, e-commerce, SEO, ads, communication
   - Video lessons with transcripts, notes, downloadable resources
   - Taught by practitioners (agency owners, consultants, entrepreneurs)

2. **10+ Master Paths (Structured Learning):**
   - Example: "AI Marketing Master", "E-Commerce Growth Master"
   - Clear progression: beginner → intermediate → advanced
   - Credential at completion (verified certificate)

3. **Learnn AI Assistant:**
   - Trained on expert course content
   - Personalized course recommendations based on goals
   - Real-time support for applying knowledge to work scenarios
   - Reduces "learning → doing" friction

4. **Verified Certifications:**
   - Complete courses, pass quizzes (≥70% score)
   - Download PDF certificate with unique ID

5. **Multi-Platform Access:**
   - **Web App:** Desktop-first responsive design for focused learning
   - **Mobile Apps (iOS/Android):** Offline video downloads, on-the-go learning
   - **Browser Extension (Chrome/Firefox):** Quick access to Learnn AI while working

6. **Progress Tracking & Sync:**
   - Track course completion across devices
   - Resume where you left off
   - See learning history and achievements

7. **Subscription Management:**
   - Self-service plans: Monthly (€29) or Annual (€299, save 14%)

## 7. User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

### Epic 1: Master Alternative Evaluation

**User Story 1.1:** As a professional evaluating a master program, I want to compare Learnn to traditional masters so that I can make a low-risk decision.
- **AC1:** Landing page clearly states: *"The continuous alternative to masters and MBAs"*
- **AC2:** Comparison table: Master (€15k, 2 years, static) vs Learnn (€299/year, continuous, updated weekly)
- **AC3:** Social proof: Testimonials from users who chose Learnn instead of master

**User Story 1.2:** As a risk-averse professional, I want to try Learnn with minimal commitment so that I can verify value before investing fully.
- **AC1:** Monthly plan (€29) available with no long-term commitment
- **AC2:** Access to full catalog immediately after subscription
- **AC3:** Cancel anytime, clear CTA on pricing page

### Epic 2: Guided Master Path Completion

**User Story 2.1:** As a professional, I want to follow a structured master path so that I don't waste time deciding what to learn next.
- **AC1:** 10+ master paths visible on dashboard (e.g., "AI Marketing Master")
- **AC2:** Each path shows: courses included, estimated time, learning outcomes
- **AC3:** User can enroll in path and track progress (X/Y courses completed)

**User Story 2.2:** As a learner, I want to earn a verified credential after completing a master path so that I can prove my competence to employers/clients.
- **AC1:** Certificate issued after completing all courses in path with passing quiz scores
- **AC2:** Certificate includes: user name, path title, completion date, unique verification ID
- **AC3:** Share to LinkedIn with 1-click, download PDF

### Epic 3: Learnn AI Work Application Support

**User Story 3.1:** As a professional, I want Learnn AI to help me apply course knowledge to my specific work scenario so that I can see immediate ROI.
- **AC1:** AI chatbot accessible from every course page and via browser extension
- **AC2:** User describes work scenario (e.g., "I need to create an AI-powered email campaign for my SaaS")
- **AC3:** AI provides actionable plan referencing Learnn courses and industry best practices

**User Story 3.2:** As a busy professional, I want Learnn AI to recommend my next learning step so that I don't waste time browsing courses.
- **AC1:** AI asks: *"What's your current role and goal?"*
- **AC2:** AI recommends 3-5 courses or 1 master path with clear rationale
- **AC3:** User can directly enroll from AI chat interface

### Epic 4: Continuous Content Updates (Master-Killer Feature)

**User Story 4.1:** As a subscriber, I want to see new courses added weekly so that I know my investment stays relevant over time.
- **AC1:** "New Courses" section on dashboard shows content added in last 7 days
- **AC2:** Email notification when new courses matching user interests are published
- **AC3:** Content refresh cadence: 1-2 new courses/week minimum

**User Story 4.2:** As a professional, I want to see course update history so that I can trust the content is current.
- **AC1:** Each course page shows "Last Updated" date
- **AC2:** Course version history available (e.g., "Updated for 2026 AI trends")
- **AC3:** Outdated content flagged for review or removed

## 8. Technical Considerations

### Architecture Overview

Microservices architecture with 15 independent services on AWS (eu-south-1):
- **Backend:** identity, checkout, billing, engagement, vod, assistant (Learnn AI), quiz, recommender, cms-v4 (Strapi)
- **Clients:** Web (React+Vite), Mobile (React Native), Extension (Vite+React)
- **Infrastructure:** AWS Lambda, ECS Fargate, RDS PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, S3, CloudFront CDN

### Key Technical Requirements

**Performance:**
- Video load time <2s (CloudFront CDN)
- Web app page load <1.5s
- Learnn AI response time <3s

**Security:**
- OAuth 2.0/OIDC (Keycloak)
- DRM protection for video content
- GDPR compliance (EU data residency)

**Scalability:**
- Support 10K→100K concurrent users
- Auto-scaling Lambda/ECS
- Database read replicas

**Integration:**
- Payment: Stripe
- Video: AWS MediaConvert/Elemental
- Analytics: PostHog, Mixpanel, Firebase
- AI: AWS Personalize (recommendations), custom assistant service

### Constraints

- Primary market: Italy (Italian language, GDPR)
- Budget: Optimize AWS costs (serverless-first)
- Team: Small engineering team (leverage managed services)

## 10. Dependencies & Risks

### Dependencies

- **Content Pipeline:** Weekly new courses from expert instructors
- **Expert Partnerships:** Maintain relationships with top practitioners
- **AWS Availability:** Platform stability depends on AWS uptime
- **Payment Providers:** Stripe for revenue collection

### Risks

**Market Risks:**
- **Competition:** Other platforms may copy positioning (mitigate: brand strength, expert exclusivity)
- **Market Saturation:** Italian market size limit (mitigate: international expansion Q4 2026)

**Product Risks:**
- **Content Quality:** Poor courses damage brand (mitigate: rigorous review, user ratings)
- **Churn:** Users cancel after achieving specific goal (mitigate: continuous new content, AI engagement)
- **AI Quality:** Poor AI responses hurt trust (mitigate: continuous model improvement, human fallback)

**Business Risks:**
- **Pricing Pressure:** Users may expect lower price (mitigate: emphasize master savings €15k→€300)
- **Cash Flow:** Subscription model requires upfront investment (mitigate: annual plans for predictability)
